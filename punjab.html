Admissions

Eligibility Criteria

Short Courses & Diplomas: Open to students who have completed Matriculation or Intermediate (FA, F.Sc, ICS, I.Com).

Degree Programs (e.g., BS Computer Science / Software Engineering): Minimum 45% to 50% in Intermediate (ICS, F.Sc Pre-Engineering, or Pre-Medical with additional Math).

Required Documents

When submitting your admission application, you generally need to attach:

  • Copies of Matric and Intermediate result cards/certificates.
  • Copy of Student's B-Form or CNIC.
  • Copy of Father's or Guardian's CNIC.
  • Recent passport-size photographs (2 to 4 photos).
